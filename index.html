Welcome

Making aerospace accessible to everyone on campus

AMGT bridges the gap between classroom theory and real engineering. We run events that teach members how to make things and invest in equipment that benefits aerospace maker projects across Georgia Tech. That way, students can focus on their ideas instead of worrying on the cost of getting the tools to build their ideas.
