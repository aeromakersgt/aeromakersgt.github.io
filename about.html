About AMGT

Our mission & team

We foster an inclusive, friendly environment between students and the larger aerospace community through hands-on projects, events, and shared equipment.

Our Mission

AMGT exists to bridge the gap between classroom theory and real engineering. We run events that teach members how to make things—from composite layups to flight hardware—and we invest in equipment that benefits aerospace maker projects across Georgia Tech.

Whether you're prototyping a drone, machining a bracket, or learning a new fabrication skill for the first time, you'll find a welcoming community ready to help you push the bounds of what can be created.

  • Inclusive workshops for all skill levels
  • Shared equipment for aerospace projects
  • Events that connect students with the aerospace community
